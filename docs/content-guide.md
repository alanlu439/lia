# Content and visual guidelines

## Identity

Use **LIA Language School** in page copy and metadata. Retain the original supplied seal and the white version in the navy footer. The centered header and prominent Request Info action are intentional.

The school details were supplied by the owner on 30 September 2026. Verify additions against an approved source. Do not infer policies or institutional claims from the original academy name printed in the logo.

## Design

- Georgia for display headings; Arial for readable body text.
- Navy, warm white, and a restrained blue-grey accent.
- Generous spacing, consistent rounded photo frames, and clear navigation.
- Smooth page dissolves and scroll reveals with reduced-motion support.
- Exactly five pages; integrate imagery into existing content rather than adding galleries.

## Photography

Use authentic supplied school photos. Each photograph should appear in only one placement across the website. Responsive `-small.webp` versions represent the same placement and are not separate photos.

Keep crops appropriate to the subject, use descriptive alternative text, and omit visible photo captions. Do not identify people without approved information. Preserve the original uploaded files outside the public website; publish optimized images without unnecessary metadata.

## Contact details

Maintain the approved contact information consistently across the Request Info page and footers. The school address links to the owner-provided Google Maps location. Verify changes before replacing phone, email, address, or hours.
