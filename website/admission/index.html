GETTING TO KNOW LIA

Faith-centered learning,
with service in mind.

LIA serves grades 1–12 through a youth training approach that brings together academics, athletics, and art. Faith comes first, character second, and talent third.

Families exploring Christ-centered education can contact the LIA Office to learn about the programs and discuss their student’s needs.

DUAL ENROLLMENT

Learning with
Lighthouse Christian Academy.

LIA’s education management includes dual enrollment in partnership with Lighthouse Christian Academy (LCA).

LCA uses the A.C.E. curriculum and provides academic records and a diploma for students who meet its graduation requirements. Contact the LIA Office to discuss your student’s enrollment and next steps.
