GETTING TO KNOW LIA

Faith-centered learning,
with service in mind.

LIA serves grades 1–12 through a youth training approach that brings together academics, athletics, and art. Faith comes first, character second, and talent third.

Families exploring Christ-centered education can contact the LIA Office to learn about the programs and discuss their student’s needs.
