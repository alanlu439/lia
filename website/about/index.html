OUR IDENTITY & PURPOSE

Faith first.
A life of service.

LIA Language School is a Christ-centered youth training center in Chiang Mai, Thailand, serving young people through academics, athletics, and art.

WHO WE ARE

Heart and skill,
prepared for service.

We use academic, athletic, and art programs to nurture faith, train character, and help young men and women discover their talents. Our purpose is to equip them with the heart and skills to serve people and the community well.

LIA serves grades 1–12. Individual learning uses Accelerated Christian Education (ACE) by School of Tomorrow, with education management through dual enrollment in partnership with Lighthouse Christian Academy (LCA).

OUR MISSION

Stewards who
serve well.

To train up young people to be competent stewards who know how to serve well.

OUR VISION

Hope, truth,
and calling.

LIA Language School exists to minister hope to entrusted lives based on the truth of the Word of God so that they may discover their calling in Jesus Christ.

OUR STORY

A vow made
over 20 years ago.

LIA’s mission is built upon a vow made over 20 years ago: to provide Christ-centered education to any soul in need. Even if it serves just one soul desperately in need of that education, the mission will continue.

LIA Language School is not defined by the curriculum it uses or confined to the space it occupies. It exists to fulfill a mission in response to God’s calling: to rescue souls.

Our desire is that every soul who passes through may be saved through Logos—the living Word of God—Jesus Christ, Lord willing.

THE LEARNING COMMUNITY

Discovering talent.
Developing skill.

Core subjects and language programs are complemented by Able Fit® (Youth Functional Fitness & Sports), art, and workshops and seminars by guest speakers.

Competition preparation includes Regional Student Convention, All Asia Student Convention, and International Student Convention.

Integrated service learning connects this development with Local School Community Service Learning and International Church Service.
