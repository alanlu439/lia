WELCOME TO LIA

Faith, character,
and a calling to serve.

LIA Language School is a youth training center in Chiang Mai, Thailand. Through academic, athletic, and art programs, we nurture faith, train character, and help young men and women discover their talents so they can serve people and their community well.

Helping young people discover their talents through academics, athletics, and art.

ACADEMICS & DEVELOPMENT

Learning with
purpose.

Individual learning uses Accelerated Christian Education (ACE) by School of Tomorrow. Education management includes dual enrollment in partnership with Lighthouse Christian Academy (LCA).

Core subjects and language programs sit alongside Able Fit® youth functional fitness and sports, art, and workshops and seminars led by guest speakers.
